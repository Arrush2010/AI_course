# Assessment Rubrics

## Per-lab rubric — 100 points
- Requirements/features: 25
- Correctness and tests: 25
- Code understanding: 15
- Validation and error handling: 15
- Documentation/reproducibility: 10
- Independent extension: 10

Suggested pass: 70/100, subject to course policy.

## Capstone rubric — 100 points
- Problem definition/user stories: 10
- Scope/architecture: 10
- Working required features: 25
- Data correctness/validation: 10
- Tests/failure handling: 15
- Security/privacy: 10
- Documentation/reproducibility: 10
- Student explanation/demo: 10

## Remediation conditions
Require correction if a project exposes secrets or personal data, corrupts data in normal use, cannot be installed from its instructions, performs destructive actions without suitable validation, or lacks test evidence.

## Oral verification
1. Explain three important functions.
2. Which test revealed a defect?
3. How does invalid input get handled?
4. Which AI suggestion did you verify?
5. What data must not be sent to an AI provider?
6. What would need to change before production use?
